# Author's Desk

**The collected works of Smoke Ascendant** — nonfiction, philosophy, and fiction, written from a lifelong practice of silence, restraint, and systems thinking.

This repository is the central archive for all books, manuscripts, and long-form writing published under the name Smoke Ascendant.

---

## About the Author

Smoke Ascendant is a self-taught systems and security practitioner and independent author based in a quiet corner of the world. The work spans technical nonfiction, philosophy, and fiction, all grounded in lived experience across Nigerian settings and a personal discipline framework — **SARRS**: Silence, Awareness, Restraint, Ritual, Structure — paired with a strict practice of logging: *if it's not logged, it didn't happen.*

The same precision and structure that shapes systems and security work carries directly into the writing: long-form, chapter-structured books, historically grounded references, and a first-person voice built to serve African and diaspora readers first.

## Philosophy
